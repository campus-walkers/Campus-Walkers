STYLE GUIDELINES

Overall look and feel of the website:
A clean, sporty and energetic website, similar to a modern running-shoe brand.

Color scheme
The main background color will be white.
The navigation bar and the footer will be a charcoal/dark-greyish black.
Orange will be used as an accent color for buttons, links, etc.

White #FFFFFF - background
Charcoal #1E2328 - Navigation bar and footer
Orange #FF5A1F - buttons, links
Light grey #F4F6F8 - seperate sections, makes a little contrast with the white.

Typography
For the headings: Montserrat, bold.
Body: Montserrat, regular.

headers: 48px MAX, 22px Min
lead paragraph: 18px
body text, buttons, nav: 16px
